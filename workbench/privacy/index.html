Workbench Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Workbench is a personal Windows desktop app created and used by Sverrir. Google Calendar access is optional and requires the owner's permission.

What Workbench accesses

With permission, Workbench uses the Google Calendar read-only scope calendar.events.readonly to read events from the owner's primary Google Calendar. It checks events for today and approximately the next day, including their titles, start and end times, and whether they are marked private or tentative. It uses this information only to show the owner's next meeting in Workbench. Private event titles are not shown in the app.

Workbench cannot create, modify, or delete Calendar events. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts, or Google Drive.

Storage and processing

Calendar information is processed locally on the owner's Windows computer rather than sent to an application server. Google authorization credentials are stored locally using Windows Credential Manager. Calendar details are not collected in a remote database.

Sharing and other uses

Workbench does not sell or share Google Calendar information with other people or companies. It does not use that information for advertising or for training AI models. It does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Workbench's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

The owner can stop using Google Calendar in Workbench and revoke its Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Uninstalling Workbench stops the app from accessing Calendar. Locally stored Workbench data can be deleted from the app's local files, and locally stored Google credentials can be removed from Windows Credential Manager.
