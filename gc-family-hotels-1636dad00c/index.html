Keep the booking—for now—but actively check two alternatives.

Decision safeguards

What to verify before switching

The biggest remaining uncertainties are specific enough to resolve with one message to each hotel and the existing Booking confirmation.

Check in your Puerto Azul booking

  1. Exact name of the parents/toddler room.
  2. Whether a door separates bedroom and living/sleeping area.
  3. Free-cancellation deadline and payment status.
  4. Whether ISK 648,000 includes every tax and fee.
  5. Walking/lift route from the room category to restaurant and toddler pool.

Message for Orquídea / Las Pitas

For 17–26 December 2026, we are four adults and one child aged two. We need one accommodation for two adults and the child, plus a separate room/unit for two adults. Can you guarantee a physically separate bedroom or sleeping zone for the child? Does half board normally include breakfast and dinner, and can dinner be exchanged for lunch for the entire stay? If not, what is the adult and child price for buffet lunch when booked on breakfast-only? Which toddler pools are heated and open over Christmas, and what are the rate’s payment and cancellation deadlines?
